# Kenney Furniture Pack (CC0)

Models converted from Kenney’s **Furniture Pack** (OBJ → GLB).

- Source: [OpenGameArt — Furniture Kit](https://opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit) / [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)

- Credit (optional, appreciated): **Kenney.nl**

See `LICENSE-Kenney-CC0.txt`.

## Scene materials (Crunchadmin)

- Props keep Kenney `baseColorFactor` palettes (Cute Furniture / game-clay readable).
- Roughness/metalness are tuned by material name (wood / metal / fabric / plant); meshes are not emissive-washed.
- Interior floor uses a soft procedural plank `CanvasTexture` (not a third-party texture pack).
